# Contract Templates

Place the models in `assets/` and consult them before drafting.

## Standard order of a contract
Recitals → Definitions → Subject matter → Obligations of each party → Price and payment terms → Term and renewal → Warranties → Liability and limits → Force majeure → Confidentiality → Data protection → Intellectual property → Assignment and subcontracting → Grounds for termination → Effects of termination → Notices → Miscellaneous → Governing law and jurisdiction.

## Types and their critical clause
| Type | Where the contract is won or lost |
|---|---|
| Provision of services | Scope, SLA and change-of-scope |
| Supply | Delivery, transfer of risk, conformity |
| Distribution / agency | Exclusivity, indemnity for clientele |
| Software licence | Scope of the licence, support, liability |
| Development | Ownership of the deliverable and acceptance |
| Confidentiality | Definition of confidential information and duration |
| Lease | Term, rent, works, guarantees |
| Share/quota purchase | Representations and warranties, indemnity, escrow |

## Rules
- One obligation per paragraph, always with an explicit subject.
- "Shall" = obligation. "May" = right/option. Do not mix them.
- Defined term: capitalised, defined once, always used consistently.
- Deadlines with an explicit unit (calendar/business days) and identified starting day.
- Any data point not provided is marked **[PENDING: ...]**. Never invented.
