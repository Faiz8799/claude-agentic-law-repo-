# Legal Risk Matrix

## Scales
**Probability**: 1 remote · 2 low · 3 medium · 4 high · 5 almost certain
**Impact**: 1 negligible · 2 minor · 3 significant · 4 serious · 5 critical (existential, criminal or severely reputational)

**Level = Probability × Impact**
- 1-4 🟢 acceptable, monitor
- 5-11 🟡 requires mitigation
- 12-19 🟠 requires action before signing or proceeding
- 20-25 🔴 blocking

## Output table
| No. | Risk | Source (clause/document) | P | I | Level | Economic impact | Mitigation | Owner | Deadline |

## Rules
1. Sort by descending level, never by the order in the document.
2. Quantify the economic impact whenever possible, with a range.
3. Distinguish **inherent risk** from **residual risk** after mitigation.
4. Every 🔴 and 🟠 risk also goes in the executive summary.
5. A risk with no action and no assigned owner is not being managed.
